Most moving checklists are just a flat list of tasks with no sense of when to actually do each
one. The timing matters as much as the task itself — booking movers the week before you move is
a very different situation from booking them a month out, and a checklist that doesn't
distinguish those two moments isn't that useful.

## 4-6 weeks before

- Research and book movers or a truck rental if needed — this window matters most during
  popular moving periods (weekends, month-end, summer), when availability tightens fast
- Start using up food that won't survive the move (freezer and pantry items)
- Begin decluttering — it's much easier to sort and donate before boxes are involved than after

## 2-3 weeks before

- Submit a formal change-of-address
- Schedule utility disconnection at the old place and activation at the new one, timed to
  overlap by a day rather than leave a gap
- Start collecting boxes and packing materials, and begin packing anything not used daily

## 1 week before

- Confirm move logistics (time, access, parking) with movers or helpers
- Pack a clearly labeled "first night" box — toiletries, a change of clothes, basic kitchen
  items, chargers — so the essentials aren't buried in a stack of identical boxes
- Do a final walkthrough plan for the old place if a deposit inspection is involved

## Move-in day

- Photograph the new place's condition immediately, before unpacking — the same reasoning as in
  [First Apartment Checklist (Free Printable)](/blog/first-apartment-checklist)
- Locate breaker panel, water shutoff, and check smoke detectors before anything else
- Unpack the "first night" box first, and resist the urge to fully unpack everything at once

## After move-in

Give yourself real time to settle rather than expecting the space to be fully organized within
the first weekend — see
[Small Studio Apartment Organization Ideas That Actually Work](/blog/small-studio-apartment-organization-ideas)
for a zone-based approach that works better tackled gradually than all at once.
