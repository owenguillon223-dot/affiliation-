Most "best elopement destinations" lists are really just pretty-photo roundups, ranked by
Instagram appeal rather than by what actually makes a location work for eloping — permits,
accessibility, season, and cost all matter more than how a place photographs.

## Sort destinations by landscape type first

**Mountains and alpine locations** — dramatic backdrops, but often require more physical
exertion (hiking to a viewpoint) and are more weather-dependent than other options; check
seasonal access, since many alpine spots are inaccessible or dangerous outside a narrow window.

**Coastal and cliffside locations** — reliably photogenic and usually easier to access than
mountain locations, but wind and tide timing matter more than people expect when planning around
them.

**Desert locations** — dramatic light, especially at golden hour, with generally more predictable
weather than mountain or coastal options, but extreme heat in summer months can restrict
practical ceremony timing to very early morning or evening.

**Urban and architectural locations** — the most accessible and least weather-dependent option,
often overlooked in favor of nature settings, and can be significantly cheaper due to lower
travel costs.

## What to check before falling in love with a location

- **Permit requirements**, especially for photography — many scenic public lands require a
  permit that has its own application timeline, separate from your marriage license
- **Accessibility for anyone joining you** — if even a small number of guests or a witness is
  coming, confirm the location works for their mobility and travel situation, not just yours
- **Seasonal access windows** — some of the most photogenic locations are only accessible or
  safe during a specific few months
- **Realistic travel cost**, not just flight cost — many remote elopement locations also require
  significant additional local transport once you arrive

## A framework, not a ranking

Instead of chasing a "best of" list, rank what actually matters to you: is this about a specific
landscape, minimizing cost and logistics, or being near family for a celebration afterward?
Whichever priority wins should determine your search, not a generic ranked list built for
engagement rather than your specific situation.
